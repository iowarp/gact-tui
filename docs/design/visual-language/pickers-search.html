When space runs out
Width follows the anchor. The model picker and the @ menu are 100% of the composer's width. Ctrl K⌘K is 50% of the window, 420 px at least and 720 px at most. Below 480 px everything is 100% wide minus 2%.
What goes first. 1. The faint detail after the name. 2. Facts other than context. 3. Two columns become one at 600 px; the provider bar becomes a manage row per group. 4. Row 2 keeps fewer chips, then folds into the Filters button's count; tabs become icons. 5. Footer keys except ↵ and Esc.
What never goes. The search field, the Filters button, the highlight, the group labels and the result count. Log in and Update stay reachable at every width, behind ··· at the smallest.

Open questions
  • Tabs or strip. Tabs are recommended. Is ten types too many for tabs, with four behind More?
  • Where the provider bar lives in one column. A manage row under each group, or one bar in the footer that follows the focused row?
  • Quick filter chips. Fixed set, or the last filters you used?
  • ← → at the end of the text. Develop's columns already work this way. Is it clear enough for tabs too?
  • Model picker key. Ctrl '⌘' is free in browsers and on a Mac. Worth it, or is Alt ↓⌥↓ on the trigger enough?
  • Downloads in the picker. Show Hugging Face in the composer picker, or keep it in Infrastructure?
