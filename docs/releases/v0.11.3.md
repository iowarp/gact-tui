GACT 0.11.3 brings the workspace, study dashboards and desktop startup changes from
the 0.11.3 previews into a stable release.

### Browse large workspaces

The workspace loads conversations and older messages in bounded pages. Open a
conversation at its newest messages and scroll back without fetching every
session in the workspace. (#691)

### Keep study dashboards with their data

A study's Views show the dashboards its conversations attached, including the
title, purpose and branch that produced each one. Charts and tables can follow
the study's measurements and entries, and exports retain the data each view
showed. (#686)

### Follow desktop setup to completion

The Windows launcher supervises protection checks and managed-package
preparation, keeping their progress and process ownership available through
completion. Desktop shutdown also retains responsibility for its backend and
installer workers. (#691, #697)

## Fixed

Opening a child conversation remains reliable after closing its canvas. The
navigation check waits for the evidence rail to return to its parent layout.
(#698)

See the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/iowarp/gact-tui/blob/v0.11.3/CHANGELOG.md)
for the changes included from the preview releases.
